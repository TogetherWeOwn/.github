# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be kind and direct. Assume good faith, disagree about the work and not the person.
- Unacceptable: harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery,
  doxxing, and any conduct that would make a reasonable contributor stop contributing.
- Enforcement: report to the repo maintainers privately. Maintainers act
  (warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) and keep reports confidential.
